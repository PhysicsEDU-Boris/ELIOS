# 0208 — Home in the L3 style, with an entrance on every open

Date: 12 November 2026. Same branch as 0206/0207 (PR #117). Owner request: make Home match the
newest Figma Home (`02 · Home`, 629:80377 light, 629:81350 dark) and give it an entrance like
Learn's when the Home tab is tapped.

## What changed against Figma

- Bold text was rendering regular: `copyWith(fontWeight:)` does not move the variable font's
  `wght` axis. Home text now sets both (`homeWeight`), using the Headline / Display / Caption tokens.
- Greeting: 44 dp surface bell circle. Search: surface fill, subtle border, 14 px hint.
- Hero: 320 high, flat dial ends, sticker at the screen edge. Pebbles 108 / 122 / 106 with line
  icons (new `icon_scan.svg`, `icon_target.svg`) and chip tints.
- Daily quests: 20 px bold heading, 96 px squiggle, middle card raised 16, reward chip in caption bold.
- Scan activity: leaf panel 32/10/32/10, green Year | Month pill, 17 × 7 heatmap without month
  or day labels, five Figma levels, 4-square legend.
- New: Recent scans → History (fan of the 3 latest History scan cards, total, latest scan,
  Open History). Hidden with no scans.
- Bin buddies: no card; bins on a wavy shelf sized by rank (1.3 / 1.05 / 0.95), count above,
  green share bars, tilted "Top" sticker.
- This week / Quiz accuracy: leaf and mirrored leaf; 12 px bars with stubs on empty days; a
  plain 2.5 px line (no fill).
- Medals: no card; medals hang from a ribbon across the screen, the middle one lower. "1 of 3 ›"
  opens a sheet with each medal's rule.
- Thumbnails (739:30806, 70 × 44 frame, cover inset with radius 7) on every search result
  (video / guide cover, simulation art, quiz art, scan photo) and on the selected day's scans.
- Removed: "Did you know?" (not in the Figma Home).

## Not 1:1

- Quest cards and leaf cards grow with long text (Figma is fixed 160 / 172).
- The fan reuses the real History scan card scaled to 36 %.
- Week bars keep the bin colours (Figma only shows an empty week).
- Icons are Material look-alikes except the two pebble icons.
- No motion spec for Home exists in Figma beyond section 13; the timings above are ours.
